Our Story & Mission

About Us

Kalakidor is more than just an art store; it's a living tribute. Founded by Ridhima Gupta, in memory of her beloved sister Navya.

“

From the depths of our hearts, thank you for believing in Kala ki Dor. Kala ki Dor is more than a brand—it is a thread of love that began with my sister, Navya. A soul so pure, she now lives on in every knot, every colour, and every creation we share with the world. Though she left us too soon, her spirit still holds my hand through every stitch.

When you support Kala ki Dor, you are not just buying handmade art. You are holding a memory. A heartbeat. A whisper of love that never fades.

Ridhima Gupta Founder, Kala ki Dor

Three Pillars of Change

Training

We provide training to artisans to empower and encourage them to grow.

Empowerment

In the past, provided skill training and dignity to women in Red Light Areas and the LGBTQ+ community.

Mental Health

Breaking taboos around depression and anxiety through awareness workshops and counseling.

Be a Part of the Movement

Whether you buy a product, volunteer your time, or simply share our story, you are helping us build a kinder world.

Quality Assurance & Policy

Return Policy

All our products are handcrafted by traditional craft persons, resulting in each product being unique and slightly different from each other.

An irregular weave, print, or stitch should not be taken as a defect, as handcrafted products will not show consistent uniformity like a machine-made product.

Despite these variables, at KALA KI DOR, we stand behind the quality of our products and we allow exchange of your purchase in certain circumstances.

⏱️

24-Hour Exchange Policy

We have a 24-hour Exchange policy, which means you have 24 hours after receiving your item to request an exchange.

📦

Unboxing Video Mandatory

We only accept returns in case of a damaged product. To be eligible for a return, you must provide an unboxing video showing you opening the package.

🚫

No Mind-Change Returns

We do not accept returns or exchanges due to reasons like change of mind, lack of utility, etc.

🚚

Self-Ship & Shipping Charges

You have to self-ship the product and we will not cover shipping charges for Return/Exchange. Address provided based on nearest office.

To start a return or exchange, or for any questions, you can contact us directly on Instagram @kalakidor.

* Please note: We are not liable for any Returns/exchanges that we were not notified about first.
